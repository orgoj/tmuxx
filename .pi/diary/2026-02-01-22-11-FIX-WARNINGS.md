## Lessons Learned

### Technical Lessons:
- **Ratatui 0.29 Changes**: The `Title` fluent API for alignment is deprecated. The preferred way is to align the `Line` passed to `title_top` or `title_bottom`.
- **Environment Tools**: `rg` is preferred over `grep` for recursive searches in this project.

### Process Lessons:
- `cargo check` is much faster than `cargo build` for verifying warning fixes.
